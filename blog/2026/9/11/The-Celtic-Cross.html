# The Celtic Cross

The Tarot.

The concept is simple. You shuffle the deck. You draw.

Every time you shuffle a deck of playing cards, you are drawing from one of 52! (opens new window) possible configurations of the deck to draw the card you see before you.

Staggering numbers to wrap your head around.

These possibilities—this array of alternate timelines you could pull from all point to the same conclusion: they are all illusions. The only one that matters is the one you pulled. That is the only one that is real to you.

# The Reality

Would the card have been different had I shuffled? Likely.

But it doesn't matter. Because that's not what happened. You shuffled. And here we are. I can only help you understand the meaning behind the cards. But it's inextricably linked to you. Your past. Your present. And your future. All led to this card presenting itself before you now.

Tell me.

What do you see?

# The Tarot

I remember when I first started getting into Tarot. I bought the Light Grey Tarot (opens new window) deck.

I thought it was interesting because it was a collaborative effort; each Tarot card is drawn by a different artist.

Note: I can't seem to find this deck anymore so I'm not sure if I still have it. Regardless, I've always enjoyed collaboration between artists, which is why I love the music and film industry.

I remember going to a house party at UCF and finding a Rider Waite Tarot deck in someone's room.

Naturally, I offered to do Tarot readings to all the girls.

This actually drained me so fast. After the first few, I was noticeably fatigued. I felt sapped of energy. I apologized; I could not continue.

What happened? Why was I so tired after a little "party trick"?

# The 3 Card Spread

To begin with, I would simply read the translations from the guidebook that came with the Tarot book. Eventually, I learned what each Tarot card symbolized and now I have a page of notes for each card where I can jot down my reflections.

I didn't bother with upside-down cards. That is a bit more complicated, where you interpret the card's energy as "blocked" and attempt to invert its meaning. I stick to face-up cards strictly.

I would start out doing a 3 card spread. Naturally, I assumed it represented the past, present and future. And it can! However, I've since realized that it doesn't necessarily have to be read chronologically from left to right.

You can use the 3 card spread to indicate the source of the issue and the 2 orbiting bodies that surround it.

Eventually, I stopped looking at it in terms of past/future and just accepted that the 3 card spread simply provides more context than the 1 card spread.

I was doing past, present and future readings for girls at that party.

That's typically how I read 3 card spreads. My perspective on it has since matured a bit and I try to interpret them as three points needed for orientation. Not necessarily to see into the future. But to discover where you are now.

# The Lovers

The first card I drew was the Lovers. A major Major Arcana. This is powerful.

I was immediately stunned. Obviously, this spread was meant to catch my attention.

Still thinking I was looking at the past, I had no choice but to keep going, feeling trepidation that the future I would reveal would not include the past I had just seen.

# The 3 of Wands

Wands are the suit of fire. Passion. I've always felt a kinship with the suit of wands because I am an Aries.

I tattooed The Magician from this same Tarot deck onto my forearm because he represents willpower. I've always felt I embody the element of fire because of my burning passion. I can harness it to create fire to destroy, to cook, or focus into a pin-point laser.

What appears as magic to you is careful planning, preparation and presentation on my part.

In the 3 of Wands, we see a marine bird diving headfirst into the waves as others follow suit.

From a great height, it was able to gain perspective and establish a strategic, forward-thinking view of the situation.

# The 4 of Cups

Cups are the element of water, representing the emotional world.

Here, 4 cups hide beneath the leaves and branches of a willow tree.

It is a time of Introspection. Apathy. Withdrawal. Dreaming. Indifference.

If you feel dissatisfied with life, stuck, or in a creative block, it is important to realize the situation and start imagining a way out.

# The Future

What we do is not fortune-telling. We are not simply "Magicians". You do not read the Tarot to predict the future.

That being said, it can be instrumental in plotting a course into the future.

Such a precision instrument is called a watch.

What if there is an underlying mechanic to the Tarot? Surely, I should figure it out, if that's what I'm going to base my source code on.

Over time, I slowly realized that these readings were always accurate. Similar to how you might check your watch to see what time of day it is; when you check, it should be accurate.

Only we simulate free agency pretty well. So how do you reconcile between my struggle to conquer this demon and the promise I made to trade with Bitcoin?

Based on my calculations, I should reach Alcyone by 2049 going slower than the speed of light, meaning it should be possible to do it physically.

And I did. I made the crossing via astral projection.

# The Time

My appointment is at 3. I look down at my watch. 2:30 PM. Great. I'll be on time.

Did I predict the future? Of course not. I just measured the present.

One of the first merit badges I took in Boy Scouts was Orientation. To orient yourself, you use your surroundings to identify your location in space and if possible your placement in time.

If you are ever disoriented... orient yourself.

# The Chance

Even if the universe was deterministic and everything happens for a reason, from our perspective, our senses are just so flooded with information from the torrential river of time that we can't help but be caught in the cause and effect loop of the eons of the past.

How then do we orient ourselfves if we're tumbling down a river?

The same way you measure gravity when you are stuck in an avalanche: you spit.

Gravity will pull it down. So whichever way the spit flows is down. Towards the Earth. Now, you can orient yourself precisely in 3D space.

A river is slightly different. You spit and it gets washed away in the current. In this case, there are two things you'd want to look out for.

Which direction are you flowing in? Close your eyes. Use your sense of inertia. Which direction is the river taking you?

Obviously this would be easier to measure the slower the current is taking you. So perhaps you wait until you get a moment of respite from the river.
