## Recipe
8 servings. 20-30 minute prep time.
LOW: 8-10 hours HIGH: 5-6 hours.

## Ingredients

<pre><code>
2   cups            dry rice (makes 2 servings or 2 cups of cooked rice, refrigerate the rest)
2   cups (1 lb)     dry black beans
1                   yellow onion (diced)
3                   Roma tomatoes (diced)
2-3                 potatoes, cubed
2-3                 bell peppers
3-4                 fresh jalapeños (optional, for heat level or spicyness)
2-3                 carrots, sliced (optional)
6   cups            water if beans are soaked; else 7 cups
6   tablespoons     Better than Bouillion (1 spoon for each cup of water. Chicken broth will do.)
                    salt
                    black pepper
                    garlic powder
                    optional spices
1   lb              frozen spinach (optional)
1-2 lbs             protein (optional)
</code></pre>

1. Soak beans overnight.
2. Cook rice 30 minutes before serving.
3. Chop vegetables and throw in pot.
4. Add all ingredients besides spinach.
5. Cook in slow cooker. LOW: 8-10 hours, HIGH: 5-6 hours.
6. Throw in frozen spinach at the last hour/half hour.
7. Add rice to bowl. Scoop 1 to 2 servings of stew on top. Optionally add cheese, hot sauce, sour cream, etc. to suppliment. Makes excellent burrito filling.
8. Eat it.
